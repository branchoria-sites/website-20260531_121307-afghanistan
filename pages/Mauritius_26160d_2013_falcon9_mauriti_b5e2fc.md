## Introduction

The most widely documented UFO report associated with Mauritius occurred on the evening of 29 September 2013, when residents of Mauritius observed a bright, expanding object in the sky that appeared unlike any familiar aircraft, star, or meteor. Similar reports emerged almost simultaneously from neighbouring Réunion and from multiple locations across southern Africa. For a brief period, the phenomenon was widely discussed as a possible UFO because of its unusual appearance: a glowing orb surrounded by a diffuse cloud that seemed to grow in size and drift across the sky.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.space.com/23013-spacex-falcon-9-rocket-ufo.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: space.com">[Space]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">space.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">X Rocket Launch Sparks &#x27;UFO&#x27; Sightings: Reports</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">SpaceX Rocket Launch Sparks &#x27;UFO&#x27; Sightings: Reports...September 30, 2013 — 30 Sept 2013 — People on the ground in southern Africa...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 30, 2013</span></span></span>

Unlike many UFO cases, however, this event was investigated rapidly and linked to a known aerospace activity. The object was ultimately identified as venting from the upper stage of a SpaceX Falcon 9 rocket launched earlier that day on the CASSIOPE mission. ## Witness Accounts

Reports from Mauritius described a luminous object appearing against the night sky, often characterised as a glowing sphere or bright cloud with a concentrated central light. Videos recorded on the island showed an object that did not resemble a conventional aircraft and appeared far larger than a typical satellite. ## Rocket Trajectory and Plume

The key to understanding the event lies in the Falcon 9 launch timeline. SpaceX launched the CASSIOPE mission from Vandenberg Air Force Base in California at 16:00 UTC on 29 September 2013. After delivering its payload toward orbit, the rocket's second stage continued travelling thousands of kilometres above Earth. During post-mission operations, the stage vented residual propellants. At orbital altitudes, released gases rapidly expand into enormous clouds that can stretch for hundreds of kilometres. ## Why the Sighting Looked So Unusual

The Mauritius case is particularly valuable because it demonstrates how an authentic and dramatic observation can still have a conventional explanation.

Most people are familiar with aircraft lights, meteors, and astronomical objects. Few have experience observing orbital-stage venting. The phenomenon combines several visual characteristics that naturally encourage UFO interpretations:

<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Apparent silence.
* Lack of visible wings or structure.
* Unusual shape changes.
