## Summary

Belize’s record of “UFO” or UAP reports consists primarily of **civilian sightings of unexplained lights and occasional structured reports**, documented through independent databases such as NUFORC and local news anecdotes. There is **no publicly accessible official archive or government investigation programme** addressing UAP in Belize. Many sightings remain **unverified and open to conventional explanations**, and while some reports are intriguing for their detail, none rise to the level of confirmed non‑earth‑technology evidence. Independent civilian reports, taken together, show that Belize participates in the broader global pattern of unidentified sighting claims, but without verifiable investigatory backing or systematic classification.
