## Introduction

The **Greifswald lights** of **24 August 1990** stand out as **[Germany]({{ 'what-germanys-ufo-record-really-shows/' | relative_url }})’s best‑known modern UFO case** because dozens of eyewitnesses across the Baltic coast photographed, filmed and described luminous aerial formations that evening — making it unusually well documented compared with typical lone reports. What makes this incident distinctive in the German UFO record is the combination of multiple independent visual records, a clear time‑window of observation, and continuing disputes over conventional explanations. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://en.everybodywiki.com/Greifswald_Lights" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: en.everybodywiki.com">[EverybodyWiki Bios &amp; Wiki]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">en.everybodywiki.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Everybody Wiki Bios &amp; Wiki Greifswald Lights</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EverybodyWiki Bios &amp; WikiGreifswald Lights - EverybodyWiki Bios &amp; WikiNovember 18, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 18, 2025</span></span></span>

## The 24 August 1990 Sightings: What Was Reported

On the evening of **24 August 1990**, at around **20:30 local time**, residents in Greifswald, Rostock, and on the Baltic islands of **Rügen** and **Usedom** saw **clusters of luminous spherical lights** in the sky above the **Greifswalder Bodden (a Baltic bay)**. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://en.everybodywiki.com/Greifswald_Lights" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: en.everybodywiki.com">[EverybodyWiki Bios &amp; Wiki]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">en.everybodywiki.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Everybody Wiki Bios &amp; Wiki Greifswald Lights</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EverybodyWiki Bios &amp; WikiGreifswald Lights - EverybodyWiki Bios &amp; WikiNovember 18, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 18, 2025</span></span></span>

Eyewitnesses described:

* **Two main groups of lights** — one appearing roughly circular, the other in a “Y‑shaped” configuration. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://mytour.vn/en/blog/lifestyle/top-10-lesser-known-ufo-sightings-with-multiple-witnesses-mytour.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: mytour.vn">[Mytour.vn]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">mytour.vn</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Discover the Top 10 Most Unbelievable UFO Sightings with Multiple Witnesses</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">MytourFebruary 15, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 15, 2026</span></span></span>
* The lights **hovering for 30–45 minutes**, visible to people in multiple towns and photographed or filmed by several independent witnesses. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://en.everybodywiki.com/Greifswald_Lights" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: en.everybodywiki.com">[EverybodyWiki Bios &amp; Wiki]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">en.everybodywiki.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Everybody Wiki Bios &amp; Wiki Greifswald Lights</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EverybodyWiki Bios &amp; WikiGreifswald Lights - EverybodyWiki Bios &amp; WikiNovember 18, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 18, 2025</span></span></span>

## Why Photos and Video Did Not Settle Things

The strength of the Greifswald case lies in its **visual documentation coupled with independent witness testimonies**. Many observers took still photographs and amateur video from separate vantage points, a feature that elevates this incident above anecdotal sky reports. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://en.everybodywiki.com/Greifswald_Lights" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: en.everybodywiki.com">[EverybodyWiki Bios &amp; Wiki]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">en.everybodywiki.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Everybody Wiki Bios &amp; Wiki Greifswald Lights</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EverybodyWiki Bios &amp; WikiGreifswald Lights - EverybodyWiki Bios &amp; WikiNovember 18, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 18, 2025</span></span></span>

Despite this, the **images and footage have not yielded a definitive explanation**:

* Some recorders show **stationary lights** maintaining form while others suggest movement inconsistent with known aircraft. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://mytour.vn/en/blog/lifestyle/top-10-lesser-known-ufo-sightings-with-multiple-witnesses-mytour.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: mytour.vn">[Mytour.vn]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">mytour.vn</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Discover the Top 10 Most Unbelievable UFO Sightings with Multiple Witnesses</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">MytourFebruary 15, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 15, 2026</span></span></span>
* Video recordings do not capture typical aircraft navigation lights or noise cues, and the lights’ apparent formations have been described as structured. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.tvi.show/skywatch-files/the-1990-greifswald-lights" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: tvi.show">[Things Visible &amp; Invisible]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">tvi.show</span><span class="citation-popover-title">the 1990 greifswald lights</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Things Visible &amp; InvisibleMystery of the 1990 Greifswald Lights: UFO Sightings Over the Baltic Sea...</span></span></span>
* No contemporaneous official radar data has been publicly released to confirm anomalous tracks in the airspace.

In UFO research circles, this leaves the Greifswald lights in a **persistent middle ground**: unusual and well documented, but not conclusively linked to any physical object beyond conventional explanations.

## Competing Interpretations and Contested Explanations

From early on, explanations for the lights have split into **skeptical, conventional accounts** and **unresolved/UAP–leaning interpretations**.

### Conventional Explanations

One of the most cited rational explanations from German UFO researchers is that the phenomenon was caused by **military illumination flares** deployed during exercises. According to this account:

* The **Czechoslovak Air Force** (then part of the Warsaw Pact) was authorised to conduct drills over the Baltic (Luftschiesszone II) on that date, using **SAB‑type illuminating bombs** that release clustered pyrotechnic flares suspended on parachutes. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.ufoinfo.de/index.php/themen/faelle/greifswald" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufoinfo.de">[ufoinfo.de]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufoinfo.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Die Greifswald UFO Lichter</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ufoinfo.de</span></span></span>
* These flares drift at altitude, glow brightly, and descend slowly — mimicking clusters of lights.

This explanation has been published in UFO‑focused German research circles as a **“solution”** to the Greifswald sighting, tying the light characteristics to known ordinance behaviour. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.ufoinfo.de/index.php/themen/faelle/greifswald" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufoinfo.de">[ufoinfo.de]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufoinfo.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Die Greifswald UFO Lichter</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ufoinfo.de</span></span></span>

### Unresolved and Skeptic‑Challenged Points

Not all investigators accept the flare explanation:

* The **Deutschsprachige Gesellschaft für UFO‑Forschung (DEGUFO)** has argued that neither the **duration** nor the **behaviour** of the lights fits typical military flares, and that no matching manoeuvres were confirmed that night. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.openpr.de/news/658020/Greifswald-UFO-Raetsel-seit-22-Jahren-ungeloest.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: openpr.de">[openPR.de]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">openpr.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">open PR.de Greifswald UFO-Rätsel seit 22 Jahren ungelöst</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Greifswald UFO-Rätsel seit 22 Jahren ungelöstAugust 24, 2012...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 24, 2012</span></span></span>

## Coastal and Military Context: Why Greifswald?
