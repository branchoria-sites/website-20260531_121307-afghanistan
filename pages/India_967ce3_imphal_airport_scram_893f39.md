## Introduction

In November 2023, the **Bir Tikendrajit International Airport** in **Imphal, Manipur** became the centre of an unusual aviation incident when an **unidentified flying object** (UFO) was reported hovering over the airfield, prompting a significant air‑defence response and disruption of flight operations. While local witnesses and security personnel described seeing something in the sky, official investigation results have remained inconclusive, and **no positive identification of the object has been confirmed** by the Indian authorities.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/imphal/ufo-sparks-3-hour-imphal-airspace-closure/articleshow/105340146.cms" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: timesofindia.indiatimes.com">[The Times of India]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">timesofindia.indiatimes.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Times of IndiaUFO: ‘UFO’ sighting shuts Imphal airspace, IAF fighter jets scramble &#124; Imphal News - Times of IndiaNovember 20, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 20, 2023</span></span></span>

## Airport Disruption and Air‑Defence Response

On **19 November 2023** at around **14:30 local time**, personnel from the **Central Industrial Security Force (CISF)** monitoring the airport reported to air traffic control that an **unidentified flying object** was visible just above the terminal area and **hovering at altitude** over the airport. Witnesses on the ground, including airline and airport staff, saw a **white object** in the sky for over an hour before it disappeared near sunset.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/imphal/ufo-sparks-3-hour-imphal-airspace-closure/articleshow/105340146.cms" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: timesofindia.indiatimes.com">[The Times of India]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">timesofindia.indiatimes.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Times of IndiaUFO: ‘UFO’ sighting shuts Imphal airspace, IAF fighter jets scramble &#124; Imphal News - Times of IndiaNovember 20, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 20, 2023</span></span></span>

Because unidentified objects near commercial airfields pose a significant safety concern, the **controlled airspace over Imphal was closed**, halting all departures and arrivals for more than **three hours**. Passenger flights were affected: some outbound services were delayed on the ground, while two inbound flights — an **IndiGo A320 from Kolkata** and another from **Delhi — were diverted** to Guwahati and Kolkata respectively, as ATC maintained a holding pattern and awaited clearance from security agencies and the **Indian Air Force (IAF)**.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/imphal/ufo-sparks-3-hour-imphal-airspace-closure/articleshow/105340146.cms" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: timesofindia.indiatimes.com">[The Times of India]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">timesofindia.indiatimes.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Times of IndiaUFO: ‘UFO’ sighting shuts Imphal airspace, IAF fighter jets scramble &#124; Imphal News - Times of IndiaNovember 20, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 20, 2023</span></span></span>

To assess potential threats and investigate the sighting, the **Indian Air Force’s Eastern Command** activated its **air defence response mechanism**. Two **IAF Rafale fighter jets** were scrambled from nearby bases to intercept and examine the object. According to defence sources, these aircraft, equipped with advanced sensors, conducted **low‑level sweeps of the area** but **did not detect or visually locate any object** upon their arrival; the first aircraft returned to base, and a second was sent with similar results.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.newindianexpress.com/nation/2023/nov/20/indian-air-force-scrambled-2-rafale-fighter-jets-to-search-for-ufo-sighted-near-imphal-2634601.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: newindianexpress.com">[The New Indian Express]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">newindianexpress.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The New Indian ExpressIndian Air Force scrambled 2 Rafale fighter jets to search for &#x27;UFO&#x27; sighted near ImphalNovember 20, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 20, 2023</span></span></span>

Operations at the airport were resumed later in the evening — around **17:50 to 18:00 local time** — after the IAF gave clearance and **flight safety authorities** such as the **Directorate General of Civil Aviation (DGCA)** authorised a return to normal air traffic management.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/imphal/ufo-sparks-3-hour-imphal-airspace-closure/articleshow/105340146.cms" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: timesofindia.indiatimes.com">[The Times of India]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">timesofindia.indiatimes.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Times of IndiaUFO: ‘UFO’ sighting shuts Imphal airspace, IAF fighter jets scramble &#124; Imphal News - Times of IndiaNovember 20, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 20, 2023</span></span></span>

## What the Rafale Search Did and Did Not Find

A central fact confirmed by multiple news reports is that **no instrument or sensor data from radar or from the Rafale jets provided a verified contact or track** matching the object seen by human observers. The object’s apparent invisibility on radar — or its extremely low radar cross‑section — was noted by airport officials, suggesting the reflecting area was **too small to register** on airport radar systems.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/imphal/ufo-sparks-3-hour-imphal-airspace-closure/articleshow/105340146.cms" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: timesofindia.indiatimes.com">[The Times of India]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">timesofindia.indiatimes.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Times of IndiaUFO: ‘UFO’ sighting shuts Imphal airspace, IAF fighter jets scramble &#124; Imphal News - Times of IndiaNovember 20, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 20, 2023</span></span></span>

The Eastern Air Command’s post on X (formerly Twitter) reiterated that the response mechanism was launched **"based on visual inputs"** from the airport, and that **“the small object was not seen thereafter”** after the air defence operation was underway. This aligns with the fighter jets’ inability to locate any target during their sorties.[www.ndtv.com]

Importantly, authorities have **not publicly released** any formal investigative findings that would identify the object definitively as a drone, balloon, atmospheric phenomenon, or any other specific class of aerial object. Reports suggest it may have resembled common unmanned aerial vehicles or similar objects in appearance, but **neither defence officials nor civil aviation authorities confirmed such classifications** in available public statements.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/imphal/ufo-sparks-3-hour-imphal-airspace-closure/articleshow/105340146.cms" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: timesofindia.indiatimes.com">[The Times of India]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">timesofindia.indiatimes.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Times of IndiaUFO: ‘UFO’ sighting shuts Imphal airspace, IAF fighter jets scramble &#124; Imphal News - Times of IndiaNovember 20, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 20, 2023</span></span></span>

## Why the Case Remains Unresolved

Several factors contribute to the unresolved nature of the Imphal airport incident:

* **Lack of Instrumental Confirmation:** The object was seen visually by multiple eyewitnesses, yet **did not produce a clear radar signature** detectable by airport or military sensors, limiting investigative data.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/imphal/ufo-sparks-3-hour-imphal-airspace-closure/articleshow/105340146.cms" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: timesofindia.indiatimes.com">[The Times of India]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">timesofindia.indiatimes.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Times of IndiaUFO: ‘UFO’ sighting shuts Imphal airspace, IAF fighter jets scramble &#124; Imphal News - Times of IndiaNovember 20, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 20, 2023</span></span></span>
* **Transient Visibility:** After dusk, the object **disappeared from view** and did not reappear in following days, reducing opportunities for follow‑up observation or data collection.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.deccanherald.com/india/ufo-near-imphal-airport-was-visible-till-sometime-before-sunset-on-nov-19-then-disappeared-centre-2811341" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: deccanherald.com">[Deccan Herald]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">deccanherald.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Source details in endnotes.</span></span></span>
* **No Official Classification:** Despite security investigations by the IAF and potential studies by the DGCA, **authoritative public reports have not concluded whether the object was a drone, weather balloon, foreign surveillance craft, or another aerial entity**.
