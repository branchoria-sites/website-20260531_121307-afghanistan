## Introduction

In [Argentina]({{ 'argentinas-ufo-stories-records-and/' | relative_url }}), the **Centro de Identificación Aeroespacial (CIAE)** is the official aerospace‑phenomenon investigation body within the **Argentine Air Force**, charged with analysing reported **unidentified aerial phenomena (UAP)** — locally referred to as *OVNIs* — to determine their actual causes and publish formal explanations. Created on **4 April 2019** through the restructuring of a long‑standing predecessor, the Comisión de Estudio de Fenómenos Aeroespaciales (CEFAe), CIAE’s mandate is to organise, coordinate and execute investigations of aerospace events, identify conventional origins where possible, and issue conclusions to relevant agencies while also responding to citizen reports with technically grounded resolutions. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.argentina.gob.ar/fuerzaaerea/centro-de-identificacion-aeroespacial" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: argentina.gob.ar">[Argentina]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">argentina.gob.ar</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Argentina Centro de Identificación Aeroespacial | Argentina.gob.ar</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Argentina Centro de Identificación Aeroespacial | Argentina.gob.ar</span></span></span>

Official yearly reports from CIAE consistently document civilian‑submitted sightings and the outcomes of technical analysis. These documents are public and structured to make transparent how reported sightings are assessed, what explanatory tools are used, and why cases are resolved as known phenomena rather than “unexplained”. This article focuses on how CIAE handles public reports, the kinds of explanations found in recent official reports, and why these resolved cases still matter in Argentina’s broader UFO discourse.

## How CIAE Handles Public Sighting Reports

CIAE’s work starts with **formal submissions from citizens**. To enter analysis, a report must include both a **testimony** (a detailed description from the witness) and some form of **evidence** such as photographs or video. Reports lacking these paired elements are typically rejected after repeated outreach to the witness for additional material. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-ar-ciae-annual-2023-informe-ciae-2023" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">intl ar ciae annual 2023 informe ciae 2023</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyCIAE Informe de Casos 2023, Centro de Identificación Aeroespacial (CIAE) / Fuerza Aérea Argentina · 2024 · UFO Transparen...</span></span></span>

Once a case meets this threshold, CIAE employs a **two‑tier classification methodology derived from France’s GEIPAN** (Groupe d’Étude et d’Information des Phénomènes Aérospatiaux Non‑identifiés). This scheme distinguishes:

* **Type A (definitively explained)** — where conclusive evidence pinpoints a mundane cause, and
* **Type B (strongly supported explanation)** — where technical analysis reliably favours a conventional explanation even if some evidence remains indirect. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-ar-ciae-annual-2023-informe-ciae-2023" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">intl ar ciae annual 2023 informe ciae 2023</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyCIAE Informe de Casos 2023, Centro de Identificación Aeroespacial (CIAE) / Fuerza Aérea Argentina · 2024 · UFO Transparen...</span></span></span>

CIAE’s approach combines established observational diagnostics with cross‑referencing tools:

* **NORAD/space‑track.org catalogues** to locate specific orbiting objects and reentries,
* **Optical geometry and photographic artefact methods** to identify lens flares, internal reflections and near‑camera blur, and
* **Aviation and drone tracking platforms** (such as Flightradar24) to reconcile sightings with civil flight paths. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-ar-ciae-annual-2023-informe-ciae-2023" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">intl ar ciae annual 2023 informe ciae 2023</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyCIAE Informe de Casos 2023, Centro de Identificación Aeroespacial (CIAE) / Fuerza Aérea Argentina · 2024 · UFO Transparen...</span></span></span>

### High‑Volume Citizen Reports with Conventional Causes

* **2023 Annual Report:** CIAE processed 82 qualifying reports. Every case received a conventional explanation ranging from **optical phenomena** (lens flares, reflections) and **biological misidentifications** (birds, insects), to **astronomical objects** (stars, planets, satellites including Starlink) and **aeronautical/drone sightings**. One case was even found to involve a **deliberately doctored photograph** but was still categorised as a known‑cause anomaly. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-ar-ciae-annual-2023-informe-ciae-2023" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">intl ar ciae annual 2023 informe ciae 2023</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyCIAE Informe de Casos 2023, Centro de Identificación Aeroespacial (CIAE) / Fuerza Aérea Argentina · 2024 · UFO Transparen...</span></span></span>
