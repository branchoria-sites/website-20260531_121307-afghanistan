## Introduction

The best-known “UFO-style” scare documented in [Liberia]({{ 'what-is-known-about-ufos-in-liberia/' | relative_url }})’s Nimba County was not centred on lights in the sky or claims of alien craft. Instead, it involved unfamiliar objects that fell to the ground and were discovered by local residents, creating anxiety and speculation before a more ordinary explanation emerged. Reports from Nimba suggested that residents encountered instrument packages associated with weather balloons, also known as radiosondes, which can appear strange when found far from their launch sites. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://gnnliberia.com/in-nimba-county-gbehlay-geh-district-citizens-say-they-are-panic-over-discovery-of-strange-objects/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gnnliberia.com">[Global News Network Liberia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gnnliberia.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Global News Network LiberiaIn Nimba County: Gbehlay-Geh District Citizens Say They...August 7, 2020 — Citizens of District #4 in Nimba C...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 7, 2020</span></span></span>

## What Residents Reportedly Found

One of the clearest public accounts came from Nimba County reports describing residents becoming alarmed over repeated discoveries of unusual objects in rural communities. According to local reporting, citizens in the Gbehlay-Geh District expressed concern after several objects descended into the area. A farmer reportedly found one such device near Zean Old Town, while earlier discoveries had already generated discussion and fear among nearby communities. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://gnnliberia.com/in-nimba-county-gbehlay-geh-district-citizens-say-they-are-panic-over-discovery-of-strange-objects/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gnnliberia.com">[Global News Network Liberia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gnnliberia.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Global News Network LiberiaIn Nimba County: Gbehlay-Geh District Citizens Say They...August 7, 2020 — Citizens of District #4 in Nimba C...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 7, 2020</span></span></span>

The timing amplified public concern. The discoveries occurred during the early period of the COVID-19 pandemic, when rumours about contamination, foreign activity and unexplained events were already circulating in many places. Residents reportedly questioned who was sending the objects and why they seemed to be landing in the district. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://gnnliberia.com/in-nimba-county-gbehlay-geh-district-citizens-say-they-are-panic-over-discovery-of-strange-objects/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gnnliberia.com">[Global News Network Liberia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gnnliberia.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Global News Network LiberiaIn Nimba County: Gbehlay-Geh District Citizens Say They...August 7, 2020 — Citizens of District #4 in Nimba C...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 7, 2020</span></span></span>

## Why Weather Balloons Can Be Mistaken for UFO-Related Objects

Weather balloons are launched around the world every day to collect atmospheric data. Suspended beneath the balloon is a radiosonde, a small instrument package that measures temperature, humidity, pressure and other conditions while transmitting information to meteorological stations. After reaching high altitude, the balloon bursts and the equipment falls back to Earth. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.noaa.gov/jetstream/upperair/radiosondes" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: noaa.gov">[NOAA]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">noaa.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">NOAARadiosondes | National Oceanic and Atmospheric...16 Sept 2025 — The radiosonde is a small instrument package that is suspended below...</span></span></span> Wikipedia Several characteristics make radiosondes frequent sources of confusion: <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radiosonde" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on wikipedia.org.</span></span></span>

### They Travel Long Distances

A radiosonde may land far from where it was launched. Winds at different altitudes can carry the equipment across large regions before it descends. Someone finding the device may have no idea where it originated. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radiosonde" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on wikipedia.org.</span></span></span>

### They Do Not Look Like Familiar Consumer Devices

The instrument packages are designed for scientific measurement rather than appearance. Exposed wiring, lightweight materials, antennas and sensors can create an impression of specialised or secret technology. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.noaa.gov/jetstream/upperair/radiosondes" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: noaa.gov">[NOAA]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">noaa.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">NOAARadiosondes | National Oceanic and Atmospheric...16 Sept 2025 — The radiosonde is a small instrument package that is suspended below...</span></span></span>

### They Often Include Parachutes

Many radiosondes descend beneath small parachutes. Witnesses observing them from the ground may see an unusual object floating down slowly from the sky, creating uncertainty about what they are watching. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weather_balloon" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Weather balloon</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Weather balloon</span></span></span>

### The Balloon Is Usually Missing

By the time the instrument is found, the balloon itself has often burst. This leaves behind only the equipment package and fragments of material, making the object's purpose less obvious. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.vaisala.com/en/have-you-found-weather-balloon" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: vaisala.com">[Vaisala]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">vaisala.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">VaisalaHave you found a weather balloon?A weather balloon filled with hydrogen or helium gas carries the radiosonde into the upper atmosp...</span></span></span>

## How the Nimba Case Fits a Wider Pattern

The Nimba reports are not unique. Around the world, recovered radiosondes have repeatedly been mistaken for surveillance devices, military equipment, foreign technology or unidentified objects. Meteorological agencies regularly publish guidance explaining what weather-balloon equipment looks like because members of the public continue to discover it and report it as suspicious. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.weather.gov/upperair/radiosonde" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: weather.gov">[National Weather Service]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">weather.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Photos of radiosondes commonly used by the NWS are shown above</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National Weather ServiceWeather Balloon Instrument/Radiosonde InformationIf you have found a National Weather Service (NWS) Radiosonde, i...</span></span></span>

The Nimba discoveries offer a practical framework for evaluating future claims of strange aerial objects in Liberia.

* Did the object contain batteries, sensors or antennas?
