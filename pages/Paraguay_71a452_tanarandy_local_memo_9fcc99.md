## Introduction

The 1998 Tanarandy UFO story occupies a different place in Paraguay’s UFO history from better-known sighting reports that are treated mainly as evidence claims. In Tanarandy, a rural community in the Misiones region associated with strong religious and artistic traditions, the reported encounter gradually became part of local memory, retelling and identity. What makes the case notable is not that it produced decisive proof of an unidentified craft. The available evidence remains disputed and largely testimonial. ## The Reported 1998 Encounter

Accounts associated with Paraguayan ufology place the central event in September 1998. According to later retellings, witnesses reported an unusual luminous object that allegedly descended or landed near Tanarandy. Researcher Ronald Maidana, one of the principal compilers of Paraguayan UFO cases, has referred in interviews and published discussions to a reported disc-shaped object and an alleged landing associated with the community.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.instagram.com/reel/DYuSB8NOGZ5/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: instagram.com">[Instagram]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">instagram.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Trece on Instagram: &quot; ¿Ovnis en Paraguay? The difficulty for investigators is that the surviving public record is thin. Unlike military-documented incidents or cases supported by extensive contemporary reporting, the Tanarandy story is known largely through later interviews, local recollections and ufology summaries. Publicly accessible material does not provide a strong archive of photographs, instrument data, official investigations or contemporaneous technical analysis. As a result, the case sits in a category common to many rural UFO reports: documented as a local claim, but difficult to verify independently decades later.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.academia.edu/39997982/Transcripci%C3%B3n_de_la_Entrevista_para_la_Revista_UFO_Realizada_por_Toni_Inajar_Kurowski_a_Ronald_Maidana" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: academia.edu">[Academia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">academia.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Transcripción de la Entrevista para la Revista UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO (Brasil) del cual también es coeditor. 1 Un hontanar de dudas emergió y... ## How the Story Became Community Memory

Many UFO reports disappear after a few news cycles. Tanarandy followed a different path. Local social-media commemorations and anniversary references show residents continuing to recall the sighting decades after the alleged event. Posts marking the twenty-seventh anniversary demonstrate that the story remains part of community conversation rather than a forgotten regional curiosity.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.facebook.com/photo.php?fbid=1314413164020581&amp;set=a.238492228279352&amp;type=3" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: facebook.com">[Facebook]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">facebook.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">🇵🇾 #Paraguay &#124; Recuerdan 27...Paraguay &#124; Recuerdan 27 años del avistamiento de un OVNI en Tañarandy Pobladores de la comunidad de Tañara...</span></span></span><span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.facebook.com/trecepy/videos/tvalaire/825361267926089/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: facebook.com">[Facebook]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">facebook.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">cto en #TvAlAirePy...</span></span></span>

A revealing feature of the memory culture surrounding the case is the way the narrative is retold collectively. Later descriptions frequently emphasise “the family that witnessed it” or “the community that remembers it”, shifting attention away from a single witness and towards a shared local experience. In folklore studies, that transition is significant. A story becomes socially durable when it is remembered not only as something that happened to an individual, but as something that happened to a place.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.facebook.com/trecepy/posts/-ovnis-en-paraguay-recibimos-al-uf%C3%B3logo-ronald-maidana-torres-investigador-que-d/1009205128360978/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: facebook.com">[Facebook]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">facebook.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">tigación acerca de los OVNIs en Paraguay, siempre con una...Read more...</span></span></span>

The story also benefits from Tanarandy’s existing reputation as a place where memory, ritual and local history are publicly performed. The community is already known nationally for large-scale artistic and religious commemorations, particularly the cultural traditions associated with Holy Week. That environment provides a natural setting in which unusual local stories can be preserved and retold across generations.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koki_Ruiz" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Koki Ruiz</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Koki Ruiz</span></span></span>

## Art, Anniversaries and Public Retelling

Tanarandy is widely associated with the artistic legacy of Paraguayan creator Koki Ruiz and the community’s elaborate visual culture. Although the UFO story is separate from the religious events that made the town famous, both operate within the same local framework: a strong tradition of transforming memory into public narrative.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koki_Ruiz" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Koki Ruiz</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Koki Ruiz</span></span></span>

This helps explain why the sighting retained visibility. In many places, UFO stories survive mainly in specialist ufology circles. In Tanarandy, the story became part of a broader landscape of local storytelling. Anniversary references, television discussions and recurring mentions by Paraguayan researchers kept the event circulating in public memory. The result is less a formal investigation than a continuing act of remembrance.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.facebook.com/events/biblioteca-nacional-del-paraguay/ovnis-en-el-paraguay-por-ronald-maidana/1528483190791689/?locale=tr_TR" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: facebook.com">[Facebook]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">facebook.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ovnis En El Paraguay Por Ronald MaidanaOvnis En El Paraguay Por Ronald Maidana; Herkese açık · Düzenleyen:; 3 Haziran 2016 Cuma, 19:30 -04...</span></span></span><span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.facebook.com/SNTParaguay/videos/-un-ovni-en-paraguayel-uf%C3%B3logo-ronald-maidana-expres%C3%B3-que-el-pasado-10-de-abril-/2021797728280325/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: facebook.com">[Facebook]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">facebook.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">un ovni en paraguayel ufólogo ronald maidana expresó que el pasado 10 de abril</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">👽 ¿UN OVNI EN PARAGUAY?😱 👀El ufólogo Ronald Maidana...El ufólogo Ronald Maidana, expresó que el pasado 10 de abril de 1651, hubo un avis...</span></span></span>

The story’s persistence also reflects a common pattern in South American UFO culture. Reports that remain unresolved often gain symbolic value.
