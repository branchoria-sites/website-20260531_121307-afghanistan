## Introduction

Indonesia’s best-known UFO photographs occupy a strange place in the country’s UFO history. They are among the most frequently reproduced pieces of visual evidence in Indonesian UFO culture, yet they remain difficult to verify in ways that would satisfy modern photographic analysis. The two images most often discussed are the 1973 Mount Agung photograph from Bali and the 1975 Cilamaya offshore photograph from West Java. Both survive largely through later retellings, enthusiast archives, and reproduced copies rather than through well-documented original negatives or complete investigative records.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.balidiscovery.com/ufos-and-flying-saucers-visiting-bali/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: balidiscovery.com">[Bali Discovery]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">balidiscovery.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Bali Discovery UFOs and Flying Saucers Visiting Bali?</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The... That does not make them worthless. Historic UFO photographs often become influential because they are visible and memorable, not because they conclusively prove anything. In Indonesia, where many older UFO reports survive only as newspaper summaries or witness stories, photographs naturally gained symbolic importance. The problem is that a photograph can only answer a limited set of questions. ## Mount Agung's tourist photograph

The most famous Indonesian UFO image is usually linked to a Japanese tourist identified in later accounts as Ryo Terumoto. According to the story repeated in Indonesian UFO circles, the photograph was taken near Bali’s Mount Agung on 17 August 1973. After the film was developed, an unusual object appeared in the frame above or near the volcanic landscape. Later retellings expanded the account by claiming that several witnesses observed the object for several minutes and that cameras reportedly malfunctioned during the sighting.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.balidiscovery.com/ufos-and-flying-saucers-visiting-bali/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: balidiscovery.com">[Bali Discovery]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">balidiscovery.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Bali Discovery UFOs and Flying Saucers Visiting Bali?</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The... Mount Agung itself contributes to the image’s cultural power. The volcano is one of Bali’s most recognisable landmarks and carries both geological and religious significance. A photograph showing a mysterious object near such a location naturally attracts more attention than a similar image taken against an anonymous background.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Agung" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mount Agung</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Mount Agung</span></span></span>

The photograph’s weaknesses, however, are substantial:

* The original negative is not publicly available for independent examination.
* Most modern viewers encounter reproduced copies rather than first-generation materials.
* Detailed camera information, exposure settings, and development records are rarely presented alongside the image.
* Witness accounts are largely known through later retellings rather than contemporary investigative files.
* There is no publicly available technical analysis demonstrating that the object could not have been a photographic artefact, model, reflection, or misidentified conventional object.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.balidiscovery.com/ufos-and-flying-saucers-visiting-bali/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: balidiscovery.com">[Bali Discovery]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">balidiscovery.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Bali Discovery UFOs and Flying Saucers Visiting Bali?</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The... A recurring problem in UFO photography is that reproduction degrades evidence. Once an image has been copied, reprinted, scanned, or circulated through magazines and websites, analysts lose access to information contained in the original material. Grain structure, edge detail, exposure characteristics, and signs of manipulation become harder to evaluate. The Mount Agung image survives primarily as a historical artefact rather than as a technically examinable piece of evidence.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.balidiscovery.com/ufos-and-flying-saucers-visiting-bali/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: balidiscovery.com">[Bali Discovery]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">balidiscovery.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Bali Discovery UFOs and Flying Saucers Visiting Bali?</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The... ### Why the Mount Agung image remained influential

The photograph persisted because it arrived at a time when Indonesian UFO interest was developing around figures such as R. J. [Salatun]({{ 'salatun/' | relative_url }}) and later civilian research communities. A visual image could be reproduced far more easily than a witness statement. As a result, the photograph became part of a shared Indonesian UFO memory even though its evidential value remained uncertain.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.scmp.com/lifestyle/article/3050459/ufo-fans-indonesia-why-extraterrestrial-life-should-be-taken-seriously" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: scmp.com">[South China Morning Post]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">scmp.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ufo fans indonesia why extraterrestrial life should be taken seriously</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Members report mysterious sightings in the night sky and...Read more...</span></span></span><span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.thejakartapost.com/paper/2021/06/08/not-a-cult-ris-biggest-ufo-community-beta-ufo-weighs-in-on-cia-report.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: thejakartapost.com">[The Jakarta Post]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">thejakartapost.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">not a cult ris biggest ufo community beta ufo weighs in on cia report</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Not a cult: RI&#x27;s biggest UFO-community BETA-UFO weighs...8 Jun 2021 — N ur Agustinus, cofounder of Indonesia&#x27;s largest UFO-centric commu...</span></span></span>

## Cilamaya's offshore image

The second major case is the so-called Cilamaya photograph, associated with the offshore Arjuna oil field off the coast of West Java. Later accounts identify the photographer as engineer Tony Hartono Rusman and place the event on 22 September 1975. According to the narrative, he observed an unusual elongated dark-red object approaching from the horizon while he was at an offshore drilling installation approximately 83 kilometres from the coast. A photograph was reportedly taken showing the object over the sea.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://candradityaa.blogspot.com/2014/02/penampakan-ufo-di-indonesia.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: candradityaa.blogspot.com">[Candradityaa]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">candradityaa.blogspot.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">penampakan ufo di indonesia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Penampakan UFO di Indonesia25 Feb 2014 — 3.UFO Cimalaya, dipotret di lepas pantai Cimalaya oleh Ir. Offshore platforms involve trained personnel, known locations, and potentially additional witnesses. Those features create the impression of a more controlled observation environment.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://candradityaa.blogspot.com/2014/02/penampakan-ufo-di-indonesia.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: candradityaa.blogspot.com">[Candradityaa]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">candradityaa.blogspot.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">penampakan ufo di indonesia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Penampakan UFO di Indonesia25 Feb 2014 — 3.UFO Cimalaya, dipotret di lepas pantai Cimalaya oleh Ir. Because the object was photographed over open water, interpretation becomes especially difficult. Distance is often impossible to establish from a single image. A relatively small nearby object can appear similar to a much larger distant one. Atmospheric distortion, haze, lens effects, and perspective compression can all complicate attempts to determine size and motion.

### The offshore setting creates both strengths and weaknesses

The offshore environment gives the Cilamaya photograph a degree of credibility in popular discussion because it suggests observers accustomed to machinery, navigation, and industrial operations. At the same time, that setting removes many visual reference points. Without fixed landmarks, analysts cannot easily estimate altitude, speed, or scale.

As a result, the photograph occupies a familiar UFO category: intriguing enough to resist an easy dismissal, but too poorly documented to support strong conclusions.

## Why neither photograph settles the Indonesian UFO question

Supporters of these photographs sometimes argue that the absence of a convincing debunking keeps them significant. Skeptics often respond that the absence of a convincing explanation is not the same thing as evidence for an extraordinary one.

The deeper issue is methodological. A photograph alone rarely proves the nature of an object. Even internationally famous UFO photographs remain controversial when key documentation is missing. Debates over well-known cases in other countries show that striking imagery can survive for decades without producing consensus.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.theguardian.com/world/2025/feb/11/what-really-happened-in-calvine-the-mystery-behind-the-best-ufo-picture-ever-seen" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theguardian.com">[The Guardian]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theguardian.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The GuardianWhat really happened in Calvine? The Indonesian examples face additional obstacles:

* The cases emerged before digital archiving became common.
* Contemporary investigative standards were inconsistent.
* Many original materials appear difficult to access or may no longer exist.

## What photo provenance would need to show

The limitations of these classic photographs become clearer when compared with the standards modern investigators would seek.

For a historical UFO photograph to carry strong evidential weight, investigators would ideally want:

1. **The original negative or original image file**

This allows technical examination rather than reliance on copies.
2. **Documented chain of custody**

Analysts need to know who possessed the image and when.
3. **Camera and exposure information**

Lens characteristics, film stock, shutter speed, and development methods can reveal ordinary explanations.
4. **Multiple independent images**

Photographs from different positions greatly strengthen reliability.
5. **Contemporary witness records**

Statements recorded near the time of the event are generally more reliable than recollections decades later.
6. **Corroborating data**

Aviation records, radar returns, meteorological observations, or other independent sources can help establish whether something unusual occurred.

Neither the Mount Agung nor the Cilamaya case publicly satisfies most of these criteria. That does not mean the photographs are fraudulent.
