## Introduction

Recent [Singapore]({{ 'what-singapore-ufo-sightings-really-show/' | relative_url }}) sky mysteries tend to follow a different pattern from the classic UFO stories of the twentieth century. Instead of newspaper reports filtered through editors and officials, modern sightings usually begin with a photograph, short video clip or social media post. Within minutes, unusual lights can be circulating across Facebook groups, Telegram chats, TikTok feeds and local news sites. What looks at first like a portal, UFO, explosion or unidentified object often turns out to be a meteor, refinery flare reflection, atmospheric optical effect or distant industrial fire.

In fact, Singapore's modern cases reveal how difficult it can be to interpret brief visual events in a densely populated, highly connected city-state. ## The 2023 Orange Vertical Light

The images spread rapidly online, prompting jokes about science-fiction portals, supernatural signs and UFOs. Several observers noted that the phenomenon appeared suddenly and then faded away after a short period. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.stomp.sg/singapore-seen/strange-orange-vertical-light-in-night-sky-spotted-at-lower-delta-and-yishun-likely" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: stomp.sg">[STOMP]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">stomp.sg</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Strange orange vertical light in night sky spotted at Lower</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">STOMPStrange orange vertical light in night sky spotted at Lower...December 12, 2023 — 11 Dec 2023 — A mysterious orange vertical sliver...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 12, 2023</span></span></span>

### Why the Light Looked So Unusual on Phone Cameras

The December 2023 sighting demonstrated how smartphone photography can exaggerate uncertainty.

Several factors contribute to this effect:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Smartphone cameras automatically increase brightness in dark scenes.
* Digital image processing can sharpen or stretch bright light sources.
* A single frame removes surrounding context such as clouds, haze and horizon references.
* Social media reposts often crop images further, making scale and direction difficult to judge.

</div>

A bright industrial flare reflected through atmospheric crystals can therefore appear much stranger in a still image than it does to the naked eye. By the time expert explanations reached the public, many viewers had already encountered only the most dramatic versions of the photographs. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.ntu.edu.sg/nie/news-events/news/detail/it-s-not-dr-strange-no-need-to-be-alarmed-by-strange-orange-light-in-singapore-sky-says-expert" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ntu.edu.sg">[Corporate NTU]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ntu.edu.sg</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Corporate NTUIt&#x27;s not Dr Strange: No need to be alarmed by</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">December 13, 2023 — 13 Dec 2023 — Prof Wang, who holds a PhD in atmospheric sciences, said such lights are usually a reflection of sunlig...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 13, 2023</span></span></span>

## The 2024 Eastern Singapore Fireball

Unlike the 2023 light pillar, the fireball seen on 24 May 2024 involved a genuinely astronomical event.

Shortly before midnight, observers across Singapore reported a bright object streaking through the sky. Videos from multiple locations captured a luminous trail moving rapidly before disappearing. Because numerous witnesses recorded the event independently, researchers had a stronger body of evidence than is available for most local sky sightings. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.channelnewsasia.com/singapore/singapore-fireball-meteor-may-24-experts-share-what-know-4366441" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: channelnewsasia.com">[CNA]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">channelnewsasia.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">singapore fireball meteor may 24 experts share what know 4366441</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">CNASaw the fireball over Singapore? The object was widely described as a fireball, a term used for an exceptionally bright meteor. Physicists from the National University of Singapore stated that the most likely explanation was a meteoroid entering Earth's atmosphere and burning up. They suggested it could have been a fragment from a comet or asteroid, although the exact origin could not be identified without trajectory data or surviving debris. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.channelnewsasia.com/singapore/singapore-fireball-meteor-may-24-experts-share-what-know-4366441" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: channelnewsasia.com">[CNA]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">channelnewsasia.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">singapore fireball meteor may 24 experts share what know 4366441</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">CNASaw the fireball over Singapore? The observed characteristics were considered more consistent with a natural meteoroid burning up at high altitude. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.channelnewsasia.com/singapore/singapore-fireball-meteor-may-24-experts-share-what-know-4366441" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: channelnewsasia.com">[CNA]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">channelnewsasia.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">singapore fireball meteor may 24 experts share what know 4366441</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">CNASaw the fireball over Singapore? ### Why This Case Was Stronger Than Most UFO Claims

The May 2024 fireball stands out because it generated several forms of evidence at once:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Multiple independent videos.
* Witness reports from different parts of Singapore.
* Consistent timing across observations.
* Expert commentary from professional physicists.
* A known natural mechanism that matched the observations.

</div>

This does not mean every detail was resolved. Scientists could not determine the exact composition or origin of the object. But uncertainty about the object's precise identity is different from uncertainty about the overall phenomenon. The available evidence strongly supported a meteor-related explanation rather than an unidentified craft. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.channelnewsasia.com/singapore/singapore-fireball-meteor-may-24-experts-share-what-know-4366441" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: channelnewsasia.com">[CNA]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">channelnewsasia.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">singapore fireball meteor may 24 experts share what know 4366441</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">CNASaw the fireball over Singapore? ## Why Modern Sightings Spread So Quickly

Singapore's digital environment helps transform brief sky events into national talking points.

A generation ago, a person might have seen a strange light and told a few friends. Today, a photograph can be uploaded immediately and viewed by thousands of people before experts have had time to evaluate it.
