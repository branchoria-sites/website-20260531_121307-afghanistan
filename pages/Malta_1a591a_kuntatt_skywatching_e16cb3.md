## Introduction

Kuntatt UFO Malta represents one of the most visible examples of how UFO interest in Malta has shifted from isolated sighting reports into organised community activity. Rather than functioning as a formal investigative body, the group operates as a volunteer-led network that encourages people to discuss unusual aerial observations, attend group skywatches, and explore ideas ranging from conventional astronomy to controversial “contact” practices such as CE-5 meditation. In a country where no major state-backed UFO research programme exists, groups like Kuntatt have become informal hubs through which sightings are shared, interpreted, and debated.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://timesofmalta.com/article/in-malta-countryside-group-meditates-make-contact-aliens.1112641" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: timesofmalta.com">[Times of Malta]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">timesofmalta.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Local proponents of CE-5 hope consciousness can help reach out to UFOs.Read more</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Times of MaltaIn Malta&#x27;s countryside, a group meditates to make contact...July 15, 2025 — 14 Jul 2025 — In Malta&#x27;s countryside, a group...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 15, 2025</span></span></span>

## Who Kuntatt UFO Malta Is

Kuntatt UFO Malta emerged primarily through social media, where it began collecting local reports, photographs, videos, and discussions about unexplained aerial sightings. The group has been publicly associated with organiser Keith Ellul, who has appeared in local media discussing UFO-related topics and community observation events.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.facebook.com/kuntattufomalta/?locale=en_GB" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: facebook.com">[Facebook]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">facebook.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO Malta (@kuntattufomalta)From all of us at Kuntatt UFO Malta, we wish you a day filled with love, laughter, coffee, and hopefully zero...</span></span></span>

Unlike established scientific organisations, Kuntatt does not present itself as a professional research institution. Its role is closer to that of a community platform. Members exchange accounts of unusual lights, discuss international UFO developments, and organise public meetups in locations with relatively dark skies. This distinction matters because many sightings circulating through the group remain anecdotal and are not subjected to systematic scientific analysis.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://timesofmalta.com/article/in-malta-countryside-group-meditates-make-contact-aliens.1112641" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: timesofmalta.com">[Times of Malta]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">timesofmalta.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Local proponents of CE-5 hope consciousness can help reach out to UFOs.Read more</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Times of MaltaIn Malta&#x27;s countryside, a group meditates to make contact...July 15, 2025 — 14 Jul 2025 — In Malta&#x27;s countryside, a group...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 15, 2025</span></span></span>

## CE-5 and the Question of Human-Initiated Contact

One reason Kuntatt UFO Malta attracted attention beyond the UFO enthusiast community is its association with CE-5 practices. CE-5, short for "Close Encounters of the Fifth Kind", was popularised by American ufologist Steven Greer and proposes that humans can establish contact with extraterrestrial intelligences through meditation, focused intention, and collective consciousness.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://timesofmalta.com/article/in-malta-countryside-group-meditates-make-contact-aliens.1112641" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: timesofmalta.com">[Times of Malta]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">timesofmalta.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Local proponents of CE-5 hope consciousness can help reach out to UFOs.Read more</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Times of MaltaIn Malta&#x27;s countryside, a group meditates to make contact...July 15, 2025 — 14 Jul 2025 — In Malta&#x27;s countryside, a group...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 15, 2025</span></span></span>

At some Maltese gatherings, participants have reportedly engaged in guided meditation sessions before or during skywatching activities. Supporters argue that such practices increase the likelihood of observing unusual aerial phenomena or experiencing forms of communication. Critics, however, note that these claims lack scientific verification and are generally regarded as pseudoscientific by mainstream researchers.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://timesofmalta.com/article/in-malta-countryside-group-meditates-make-contact-aliens.1112641" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: timesofmalta.com">[Times of Malta]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">timesofmalta.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Local proponents of CE-5 hope consciousness can help reach out to UFOs.Read more</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Times of MaltaIn Malta&#x27;s countryside, a group meditates to make contact...July 15, 2025 — 14 Jul 2025 — In Malta&#x27;s countryside, a group...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 15, 2025</span></span></span>

The controversy surrounding CE-5 illustrates a recurring tension within UFO culture. Some participants approach the events as spiritual or consciousness-based experiences, while others attend simply out of curiosity about unexplained objects in the sky. This means that a single gathering can include people with very different expectations:

* Amateur observers interested in unusual aerial activity.
* UFO enthusiasts who believe non-human intelligence may be present.
* Curious members of the public seeking a social experience.
* Sceptics who attend to evaluate claims for themselves.
* Participants interested in meditation or consciousness studies.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://timesofmalta.com/article/in-malta-countryside-group-meditates-make-contact-aliens.1112641" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: timesofmalta.com">[Times of Malta]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">timesofmalta.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Local proponents of CE-5 hope consciousness can help reach out to UFOs.Read more</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Times of MaltaIn Malta&#x27;s countryside, a group meditates to make contact...July 15, 2025 — 14 Jul 2025 — In Malta&#x27;s countryside, a group...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 15, 2025</span></span></span>

## What Participants Claim to See

Accounts associated with Kuntatt skywatching sessions often involve lights moving unexpectedly, luminous objects changing direction, or aerial phenomena that observers feel differ from aircraft, satellites, or drones. Organisers have stated that participants are encouraged to distinguish ordinary objects from potentially anomalous ones before drawing conclusions.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://timesofmalta.com/article/in-malta-countryside-group-meditates-make-contact-aliens.1112641" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: timesofmalta.com">[Times of Malta]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">timesofmalta.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Local proponents of CE-5 hope consciousness can help reach out to UFOs.Read more</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Times of MaltaIn Malta&#x27;s countryside, a group meditates to make contact...July 15, 2025 — 14 Jul 2025 — In Malta&#x27;s countryside, a group...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 15, 2025</span></span></span>
