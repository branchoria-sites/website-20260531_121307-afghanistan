## Introduction

Most reported UFOs in [Germany]({{ 'what-germanys-ufo-record-really-shows/' | relative_url }}) do not remain mysterious for long. Once investigators compare witness descriptions with astronomy data, satellite paths, aircraft traffic, weather conditions and optical effects, the majority turn out to be ordinary phenomena seen under unusual conditions. The organisation most associated with this process is CENAP, the Central Research Network for Extraordinary Aerospace Phenomena, a volunteer-run German UFO reporting and investigation network founded in the 1970s. Its work has become increasingly visible as Germany experiences recurring waves of reports linked to Starlink satellites, bright planets, drones, atmospheric reflections and social-media amplification. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.deutschland.de/en/topic/knowledge/ufos-over-germany-hansjuergen-koehler-and-cenap" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: deutschland.de">[Deutschland]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">deutschland.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFOs over Germany: An expert provides clarity</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">DeutschlandUFOs over Germany: An expert provides clarityNovember 29, 2024 — 29 Nov 2024 — UFOs over Germany: Read on to learn how Hansjür...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 29, 2024</span></span></span> <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centrales_Erforschungsnetz_au%C3%9Fergew%C3%B6hnlicher_Himmelsph%C3%A4nomene" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Centrales Erforschungsnetz außergewöhnlicher Himmelsphänomene</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Centrales Erforschungsnetz außergewöhnlicher Himmelsphänomene</span></span></span> What makes CENAP notable is not that it“solves” every case, but that it treats UFO reports as a practical identification problem rather than proof of alien visitation. ## How CENAP investigates German sighting reports

CENAP was founded in 1976 by Hansjürgen Köhler and the late Werner Walter. Unlike groups that begin from the assumption that unexplained objects are likely extraterrestrial craft, CENAP approaches reports as unidentified observations that may eventually prove ordinary. The organisation operates through volunteer investigators, public hotlines, email submissions and an archive of thousands of historical reports. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centrales_Erforschungsnetz_au%C3%9Fergew%C3%B6hnlicher_Himmelsph%C3%A4nomene" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Centrales Erforschungsnetz außergewöhnlicher Himmelsphänomene</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Centrales Erforschungsnetz außergewöhnlicher Himmelsphänomene</span></span></span>

The core method is comparative elimination. Investigators typically ask:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* What exact time and direction was the object seen?
* Was the witness moving or stationary?
* Were astronomical objects visible at the same azimuth?
* Were satellites or aircraft crossing the area?
* Were there weather effects such as haze, inversion layers or ice crystals?
* Did the witness record video or photographs?
* Were there multiple independent witnesses?

</div>

This sounds straightforward, but many German reports arrive with incomplete information. Witnesses often estimate distances incorrectly, confuse hovering lights with stationary stars, or interpret perspective effects as rapid movement. A light low on the horizon can appear to “follow” a moving car. A satellite flare may look like an object accelerating away. Aircraft landing lights approaching directly toward the observer can appear motionless for several minutes before suddenly changing direction. These are recurring patterns in German case files. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.deutschland.de/en/topic/knowledge/ufos-over-germany-hansjuergen-koehler-and-cenap" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: deutschland.de">[Deutschland]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">deutschland.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFOs over Germany: An expert provides clarity</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">DeutschlandUFOs over Germany: An expert provides clarityNovember 29, 2024 — 29 Nov 2024 — UFOs over Germany: Read on to learn how Hansjür...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 29, 2024</span></span></span>

CENAP investigators also rely heavily on cooperation with amateur astronomers, observatories and flight-tracking tools. According to reporting by deutschland.de, the organisation has even assisted German air traffic personnel and police officers who initially could not identify what they were seeing. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.deutschland.de/en/topic/knowledge/ufos-over-germany-hansjuergen-koehler-and-cenap" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: deutschland.de">[Deutschland]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">deutschland.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFOs over Germany: An expert provides clarity</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">DeutschlandUFOs over Germany: An expert provides clarityNovember 29, 2024 — 29 Nov 2024 — UFOs over Germany: Read on to learn how Hansjür...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 29, 2024</span></span></span>

## Why Starlink transformed German UFO reporting

The sharp rise in German UFO reports after 2019 has less to do with new unexplained craft than with changes in the sky itself. The biggest single driver has been the rapid expansion of satellite constellations, especially SpaceX Starlink launches. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.uapcheck.com/news/id/3260/2025-highest-number-of-uap-reports-from-germany/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uapcheck.com">[UAP Check]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uapcheck.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UAP Check2025: Highest number of UAP reports from Germany14 Jan 2026 — The German UFO reporting center CENAP recorded a new high last yea...</span></span></span>

Before satellite megaconstellations, many Germans rarely noticed moving artificial objects in orbit except during occasional International Space Station passes. Starlink changed that dramatically. Newly launched satellites often appear as bright chains moving in formation across the night sky, creating what witnesses describe as “strings of pearls”, silent convoys or coordinated fleets of lights. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.deutschland.de/en/topic/knowledge/ufos-over-germany-hansjuergen-koehler-and-cenap" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: deutschland.de">[Deutschland]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">deutschland.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFOs over Germany: An expert provides clarity</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">DeutschlandUFOs over Germany: An expert provides clarityNovember 29, 2024 — 29 Nov 2024 — UFOs over Germany: Read on to learn how Hansjür...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 29, 2024</span></span></span>

For observers unfamiliar with orbital launches, the effect can look extraordinary:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* The lights move silently.
* They maintain regular spacing.
* They appear suddenly after sunset.
* They can vanish as they enter Earth’s shadow.
* Their movement does not resemble conventional aircraft.

</div>

In Germany, waves of reports frequently follow widely shared social-media videos or news coverage about satellites, drones or unidentified aerial phenomena. This creates a feedback cycle: more people look at the sky expecting something unusual, and more ordinary objects become interpreted as UFOs.

CENAP reported that Starlink-related sightings became one of the dominant categories in its modern files. Reports also increased because people now routinely carry smartphones and immediately upload sightings online rather than dismissing them privately. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.uapcheck.com/news/id/3260/2025-highest-number-of-uap-reports-from-germany/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uapcheck.com">[UAP Check]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uapcheck.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UAP Check2025: Highest number of UAP reports from Germany14 Jan 2026 — The German UFO reporting center CENAP recorded a new high last yea...</span></span></span> <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.geo.de/wissen/weltall/ufo-meldestelle-verzeichnete-2025-neuen-rekord-37014016.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: geo.de">[2geo.de]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">geo.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ufo meldestelle verzeichnete 2025 neuen rekord 37014016</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Das Centrale Erforschungsnetz außergewöhnlicher...Read more...</span></span></span>

## Germany’s drone era and the return of low-altitude UFO scares

Recent German UFO waves increasingly involve drones rather than distant lights in space. Public anxiety about surveillance drones, military activity and unidentified objects near infrastructure has blurred the line between legitimate security concerns and classic UFO reporting.

## What CENAP’s high explanation rate actually means

CENAP’s sceptical reputation sometimes leads to two opposite misunderstandings. Critics claim the organisation dismisses everything automatically, while some believers argue that any unresolved case proves extraordinary technology. Neither interpretation matches how most case investigation actually works.

A high clarification rate mainly shows that most reported sky anomalies have ordinary causes once enough information is available. It does not automatically prove that every sighting has been solved. In many cases, investigators simply obtain enough contextual data to identify a likely explanation with reasonable confidence. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.deutschland.de/en/topic/knowledge/ufos-over-germany-hansjuergen-koehler-and-cenap" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: deutschland.de">[Deutschland]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">deutschland.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFOs over Germany: An expert provides clarity</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">DeutschlandUFOs over Germany: An expert provides clarityNovember 29, 2024 — 29 Nov 2024 — UFOs over Germany: Read on to learn how Hansjür...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 29, 2024</span></span></span>
