## Introduction

The **Libreville 1963 encounter** — often summarised as an alleged UFO landing witnessed by a lone fisherman on **25 December 1963 in Libreville, [Gabon]({{ 'what-do-gabons-ufo-stories-really-show/' | relative_url }})** — occupies a peculiar place in Gabon’s UFO lore. Unlike the handful of better‑documented aerial sightings in other parts of the world, this account survives **only through later cataloguing by UFO enthusiasts and secondary literature**, not through any known contemporary Gabonese news, official record, or eyewitness interview from the time. Its source trail exemplifies how a claim can enter fringe literature and then be echoed for decades without verifiable documentation.[ufologie.patrickgross.org]

## The Reported Fisherman Encounter

According to UFO landing catalogues circulated in ufology circles, **a fisherman in Libreville purportedly saw an unidentified craft land at night on Christmas Day in 1963**. The account describes a **humanoid figure emerging from the craft**, uttering unintelligible sounds, leaving footprints in the sand, and then returning to the object before it departed. This narrative appears in case lists under the identifier often quoted as **Magonia 592** or **URECAT‑000096**.[ufologie.patrickgross.org]

Key elements attributed to this account in secondary sources include:

* **Single witness:** A solitary fisherman.
* **Landing and entity:** A machine is said to have landed and a humanoid figure exited.
* **Behaviour described:** The figure allegedly produced unfamiliar sounds and left footprints on the beach.
* **Departure:** The being reportedly returned to the craft, which then flew away.
* **Date and place:** Night of **25 December 1963 in Libreville**.
* **No photographs or drawings** are reported.ufologie.patrickgross.org <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://ufologie.patrickgross.org/ce3/1963-12-25-gabon-librevillef.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[ufologie.patrickgross.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Les OVNIS vus de près: URECAT-000096 - 25 décembre 1963, Libreville, Libreville, Gabon, un pêcheurJanuary 30, 2007...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 30, 2007</span></span></span>

## How the Story Entered UFO Catalogues

The earliest appearance traced in ufology literature is through a cataloguing effort that draws on older UFO compilations such as those associated with the French‑language literature of the late **1960s and early 1970s**, especially the work of French ufologist **Jacques Vallée**. Vallée’s influential book *Passport to Magonia* and its associated catalogs included hundreds of alleged landing cases from around the world, including the Libreville entry for 25 December 1963.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://medium.com/%40hfwnbq/passport-to-magonia-b9fea8bb0740" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: medium.com">[Medium]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">medium.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">PASSPORT TO MAGONIA</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">On UFOs, Folklore, and Parallel...April 4, 2026 — The second half of the book is a catalog — &#x27;A Century of UFO Landings&#x27; — containing se...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 4, 2026</span></span></span>

In the catalogues maintained by later researchers (such as the **URECAT** database), the case is indexed with minimal detail and is attributed — often only by reference — to earlier UFO literature, specifically citations like “147; LDLN 70,” pointing to editions of *Le Dernier Lumières dans la Nuit* (a long‑running French UFO magazine).[ufologie.patrickgross.org]

1. **Original mention in French UFO periodicals:** *Le Dernier Lumières dans la Nuit* (LDLN) is cited as a source line in older catalogs, though **copies of the original article or its equivalent are not readily accessible** in searchable archives.[ufologie.patrickgross.org]
