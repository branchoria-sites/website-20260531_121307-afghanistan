## Assessing Evidence Quality: Confirmed, Contested, and Debunked

**Confirmed:** Luxembourg has documented reports in international databases and historical press accounts of unidentified aerial sightings that remain **unexplained in public records** (e.g., modern NUFORC cases).

**Contested:** Some sightings, such as the 1980s luminous objects, lack corroborating data like radar tracks or official investigation reports, placing them in a category of **contested eyewitness reports** without physical evidence.

**Debunked/Common Explanations:** Many modern sightings (e.g., green fireballs, streaks of light) that initially generate speculation are **consistent with meteors, satellites or atmospheric phenomena** once analysed by experts or through meteor data.
