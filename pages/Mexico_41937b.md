## Earliest Documented Sightings and Historical Context

The earliest noteworthy recorded UFO observation in Mexico dates back to **1883**, when Mexican astronomer José Bonilla photographed over 300 dark, undefined objects crossing the Sun during solar observations at the Zacatecas Observatory. ## Notable Incidents and Claim Patterns

### Coyame Incident (1974)

One of the most prominent episodes associated with UFO lore in Mexico is the so‑called **Coyame incident** near Chihuahua in August 1974. According to UFO proponents, a mid‑air collision occurred between a UFO and a small aircraft, leading to a military recovery of debris and an alleged cover‑up. ### 1991 Eclipse Mass Sightings
