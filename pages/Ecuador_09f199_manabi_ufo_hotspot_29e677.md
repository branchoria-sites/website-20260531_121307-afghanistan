## Introduction

The coastal province of *Manab* has emerged in Ecuadors UFO discourse as a focal point for claimed sightings, speculative narratives and local folklore linked to unidentified aerial phenomena (UAP or OVNI, *Objeto Volador No Identificado*). Unlike many accounts scattered across Andean or Amazonian Ecuador, the Manab reports cluster around specific seaside localities  particularly *Crucita*, *Baha de Carquez* and *Chirije*  where civilian witnesses, ufologists and secondary reportage tie unusual lights, aerial objects and even supposed underwater installations to the broader national UFO record. ## Manabs Geography and the UFO Narrative

Manab is a long, low-lying coastal province on Ecuadors Pacific shore. Its shoreline incorporates popular beaches like *Crucita*  a tourism and paragliding centre west of Portoviejo  and nearby urban hubs such as *Baha de Carquez*, a maritime city on the Chone River estuary. These communities have robust tourist and fishing economies, with broad horizons over the open sea and coastal skies. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crucita" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on wikipedia.org.</span></span></span>

For proponents of UFO phenomena, this coastal geography is part of Manabs appeal in the national imagination: the open ocean and relatively uncluttered nocturnal sky provide a backdrop that makes unusual lights or unidentified objects more easily noticed and shared in local media. Dozens of civilian reports and socialmedia posts describe bright, erratic lights over or near the ocean around Baha de Carquez and Crucita, often photographed or animated in local news articles. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.extra.ec/noticia/ecuador/ovni-con-forma-de-cigarrillo-aparece-en-el-cielo-de-bahia-de-caraquez-137984.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: extra.ec">[Diario Extra]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">extra.ec</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Diario ExtraOVNI con forma de cigarrillo aparece en el cielo de Baha de CarquezOctober 1, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 1, 2025</span></span></span>

## Civilian Sightings: Lights, Shapes, and the Coast

Local reportage from 2025 and prior years includes several civilian sightings that have helped sustain Manabs reputation as a UFO focal point. In *Baha de Carquez* in mid2025, a resident described observing an object with a cigarlike shape moving erratically in the night sky near the beach, with intermittent intense flashes and apparent size changes over about ten minutes before disappearing into clouds. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.extra.ec/noticia/ecuador/ovni-con-forma-de-cigarrillo-aparece-en-el-cielo-de-bahia-de-caraquez-137984.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: extra.ec">[Diario Extra]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">extra.ec</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Diario ExtraOVNI con forma de cigarrillo aparece en el cielo de Baha de CarquezOctober 1, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 1, 2025</span></span></span>

Earlier accounts in the same area describe spherical or erratically moving objects emitting rapid movements and flashes of light in the sky, with witnesses recording unusual motions not readily attributable to conventional aircraft or known meteorological phenomena. These accounts are typically reported in regional news and social media, but like many UFO narratives, they remain personal testimonies without corroborating radar, photographic analysis, or independent verification. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.extra.ec/noticia/ecuador/ovni-con-forma-de-cigarrillo-aparece-en-el-cielo-de-bahia-de-caraquez-137984.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: extra.ec">[Diario Extra]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">extra.ec</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Diario ExtraOVNI con forma de cigarrillo aparece en el cielo de Baha de CarquezOctober 1, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 1, 2025</span></span></span>

## Pilot and Ufologist Claims: Submarine Structures and Military Testimony

Beyond civilian sightings, more elaborate claims have been advanced by Ecuadorian ufologist **Jaime Rodrguez**, a longstanding figure in the countrys UFO research community. Rodrguez has repeatedly asserted that a series of reports and expedientes linked to the late2000s **Comisin Ecuatoriana para la Investigacin del Fenmeno OVNI (CEIFO)** include testimony from military personnel about unusual phenomena off the Manab coast. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.eluniverso.com/noticias/internacional/el-ufologo-jaime-rodriguez-dice-que-en-ecuador-ha-conocido-de-al-menos-44-casos-de-avistamientos-de-ovnis-nota//" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: eluniverso.com">[El Universo]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">eluniverso.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">El UniversoEl uflogo Jaime Rodrguez dice que en Ecuador ha conocido de al menos 44 casos de avistamientos de ovnis &#124; Internacional &#124; No...</span></span></span>

A particularly vivid version of this narrative involves the alleged detection of a base submarina  an underwater hangar or structure of nonhuman origin  purportedly observed some 50 nautical miles off *Crucita*. Rodrguez attributes the account to a highranking naval officials sonar readings during submarine operations, and to documents he claims were among the 44 cases the commission investigated before its activities waned around 2007. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.eltelegrafo.com.ec/noticias/tendencias/213/crucita-vuelve-debate-ovni-base-submarina-manabi" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: eltelegrafo.com.ec">[El Telgrafo]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">eltelegrafo.com.ec</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">El TelgrafoEl Telgrafo - Crucita vuelve al debate OVNI: versin de base submarina reaviva la polmica en ManabFebruary 19, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 19, 2026</span></span></span>

This has been repeated in multiple local news stories, though often with caveats noting a lack of independently verified documentation. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.eldiario.ec/ecuador/existe-una-base-extraterrestre-submarina-frente-a-las-costas-de-crucita-el-ufologo-jaime-rodriguez-revelo-detalles-sobre-la-provincia-de-manabi-27022026/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: eldiario.ec">[El Diario]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">eldiario.ec</span><span class="citation-popover-title">El Diario Revelaciones de uflogo Jaime Rodrguez sobre Crucita</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">El DiarioRevelaciones de uflogo Jaime Rodrguez sobre CrucitaFebruary 27, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 27, 2026</span></span></span>

## Chirije, Tourism, and Local Memory

The small coastal area of *Chirije*, near Baha de Carquez, has occasionally surfaced in UFO discourse as part of the local tourism narrative. Articles and discussions mention tourism OVNI in the context of beachside observation spots where visitors might watch unusual aerial lights or participate in UFOthemed events. These descriptions often blend local folklore with scenic coastal appeal, and attract interest from enthusiasts projecting meaning onto the geographic setting rather than from scientific observation campaigns. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.eltelegrafo.com.ec/noticias/tendencias/213/crucita-vuelve-debate-ovni-base-submarina-manabi" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: eltelegrafo.com.ec">[El Telgrafo]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">eltelegrafo.com.ec</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">El TelgrafoEl Telgrafo - Crucita vuelve al debate OVNI: versin de base submarina reaviva la polmica en ManabFebruary 19, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 19, 2026</span></span></span>

The persistence of such narratives  sightings, speculative military links, divergent testimonies  has given Manab a distinct position within Ecuadors UFO culture. Even so, expert voices emphasise that conventional explanations (atmospheric phenomena, humanmade craft, observational error) remain much more parsimonious absent robust physical evidence.
