## Introduction

Türkiye’s UFO culture was built less by government disclosure than by a network of private researchers, conferences, television appearances, museums and sustained media attention. At the centre of that ecosystem stood the Sirius UFO Space Sciences Research Center, founded by Haktan Akdoğan in the late 1990s. While many countries’ UFO debates have been shaped by military archives or official investigations, the Turkish conversation developed through advocacy organisations, highly publicised sighting claims and mainstream news coverage that brought UFO discussions into public life. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.aa.com.tr/en/turkey/ufos-continue-to-fascinate-in-turkey-and-around-the-world/33044" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aa.com.tr">[Anadolu Ajansı]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aa.com.tr</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ufos continue to fascinate in turkey and around the world</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Anadolu AjansıUFOs continue to fascinate in Turkey and around the world24 Jun 2015 — A well-known Turkish ufologist and a vigorous advoca...</span></span></span>

Understanding Türkiye’s UFO record therefore requires looking not only at individual sightings but also at the institutions that promoted them. Sirius helped create a national audience for UFO stories, organised major conferences, established a museum, cultivated international connections and presented itself as a bridge between Turkish witnesses and the global UFO movement. Supporters viewed these efforts as pioneering research; critics argued that they often encouraged extraordinary interpretations before alternative explanations had been fully examined. ## Sirius and the rise of organised UFO advocacy

The Sirius UFO Space Sciences Research Center emerged during a period when UFO interest in Türkiye was fragmented and largely confined to local reports and small enthusiast circles. Akdoğan described the organisation as a research centre intended to connect with international UFO groups and collect reports from witnesses, pilots and other observers. The centre openly promoted the view that extraterrestrial intelligence exists and that UFO phenomena deserve serious attention. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.aa.com.tr/en/turkey/ufos-continue-to-fascinate-in-turkey-and-around-the-world/33044" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aa.com.tr">[Anadolu Ajansı]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aa.com.tr</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ufos continue to fascinate in turkey and around the world</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Anadolu AjansıUFOs continue to fascinate in Turkey and around the world24 Jun 2015 — A well-known Turkish ufologist and a vigorous advoca...</span></span></span>

This advocacy role distinguished Sirius from a neutral investigative body. Public statements by Akdoğan frequently went beyond the cautious language used by scientific institutions, presenting UFOs as a genuine phenomenon and arguing that humanity was not alone in the universe. Anadolu Agency's profile of the organisation reflected this orientation, describing Akdoğan as a prominent advocate of extraterrestrial life rather than an official investigator. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.aa.com.tr/en/turkey/ufos-continue-to-fascinate-in-turkey-and-around-the-world/33044" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aa.com.tr">[Anadolu Ajansı]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aa.com.tr</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ufos continue to fascinate in turkey and around the world</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Anadolu AjansıUFOs continue to fascinate in Turkey and around the world24 Jun 2015 — A well-known Turkish ufologist and a vigorous advoca...</span></span></span>

The centre's importance lay not simply in collecting reports but in creating infrastructure around the subject. Sirius maintained archives, publicised sightings, worked with international UFO networks and positioned itself as the primary destination for Turkish witnesses who believed they had encountered something unusual. In a country without a major public UFO archive, that gave the organisation considerable influence over which cases received national attention. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.aa.com.tr/en/turkey/ufos-continue-to-fascinate-in-turkey-and-around-the-world/33044" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aa.com.tr">[Anadolu Ajansı]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aa.com.tr</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ufos continue to fascinate in turkey and around the world</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Anadolu AjansıUFOs continue to fascinate in Turkey and around the world24 Jun 2015 — A well-known Turkish ufologist and a vigorous advoca...</span></span></span>

## Turning UFOs into a public spectacle

One of Sirius's most significant achievements was transforming UFO interest from a niche hobby into a visible public phenomenon. Rather than limiting activity to newsletters or specialist meetings, the organisation embraced public outreach.

Several initiatives illustrate this approach:

* International UFO congresses featuring foreign speakers and well-known figures from the global UFO community.
* Media appearances by Akdoğan and other advocates on national television and radio.
* Public exhibitions and educational events.
* Extensive promotion of notable Turkish UFO cases.
* Outreach aimed at universities and younger audiences. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.irishtimes.com/news/ufo-spotting-turkish-night-watchman-regards-aliens-as-the-world-s-policemen-1.789220" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: irishtimes.com">[The Irish Times]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">irishtimes.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">WITH HIS neatly clipped moustache and checked shirt buttoned up...Read more...</span></span></span> <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://eurasianet.org/turkey-ufo-believers-seek-to-shed-quack-status" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: eurasianet.org">[Eurasianet Akdo]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">eurasianet.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">turkey ufo believers seek to shed quack status</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Turkey: UFO Believers Seek to Shed &quot;Quack&quot; Status22 Jun 2009 — Haktan Akdogan, the organizer of the International UFO and New A...</span></span></span>ğan himself became a recognisable media figure. ## The conference boom and mainstream attention

The late 2000s marked the high point of Türkiye's media-driven UFO culture. International UFO and New Age congresses organised by Sirius drew speakers from multiple countries and generated significant press coverage. According to contemporary reporting, more than 1,000 attendees paid to participate in one major Istanbul conference in 2009, demonstrating that UFO discussion had become commercially viable and culturally visible. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.irishtimes.com/news/ufo-spotting-turkish-night-watchman-regards-aliens-as-the-world-s-policemen-1.789220" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: irishtimes.com">[The Irish Times]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">irishtimes.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">WITH HIS neatly clipped moustache and checked shirt buttoned up...Read more...</span></span></span>

This conference culture mattered because it gave UFO narratives legitimacy through visibility. A claim presented before a thousand attendees, covered by newspapers and discussed on television could rapidly move from a local story to a national conversation.

## The Kumburgaz effect

No single case did more to amplify Sirius's influence than the [Kumburgaz footage]({{ 'kumburgaz/' | relative_url }}) recorded between 2007 and 2009. Although the footage remains heavily disputed, its cultural impact is easier to establish than its evidential value.

Akdoğan and Sirius promoted the videos as a landmark event in Turkish UFO history. Contemporary reports quoted him describing the footage as a turning point for UFO studies in the country and evidence that authorities could no longer ignore the phenomenon. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://eurasianet.org/turkey-ufo-believers-seek-to-shed-quack-status" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: eurasianet.org">[Eurasianet]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">eurasianet.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">turkey ufo believers seek to shed quack status</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Turkey: UFO Believers Seek to Shed &quot;Quack&quot; Status22 Jun 2009 — Haktan Akdogan, the organizer of the International UFO and New A...</span></span></span>

The importance of [Kumburgaz]({{ 'kumburgaz/' | relative_url }}) was not merely that a witness filmed unusual lights or shapes. The footage arrived at a moment when Sirius already possessed a network capable of publicising it. Television programmes, conferences, websites and international UFO organisations helped circulate the recordings far beyond their original local context. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://eurasianet.org/turkey-ufo-believers-seek-to-shed-quack-status" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: eurasianet.org">[Eurasianet]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">eurasianet.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">turkey ufo believers seek to shed quack status</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Turkey: UFO Believers Seek to Shed &quot;Quack&quot; Status22 Jun 2009 — Haktan Akdogan, the organizer of the International UFO and New A...</span></span></span>
